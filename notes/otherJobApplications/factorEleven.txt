	> Describe the principles of an event-driven architecture. Which approach would you use to communicate among decoupled services?

	Event-driven architecture is a software design pattern in which decoupled services or components communicate by emitting and responding to events. To communicate with decoupled services one typically uses a message broker or an event bus, e.g. Apache Kafka, RabbitMG, Google Pub/Sub, etc.

	> Describe what a Promise is in Node.js. Do you know at least two ways to wait for the completion of a Promise?

	A promise in Node.js is an object representing the eventual completion or failure of an asynchronous operation and its resulting value. To wait for the completion of a promise, you can either use the then/catch or async/await syntax.
