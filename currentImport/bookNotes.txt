We are in the midst of a shift in human history, where the primary work of mankind is moving from industrial-age control to knowledge-age release. The industrial-age module was developed during a peiod when mankind's primary work was physical. Consequently, it's optimized for extracting physical work from humans. But while you may be able to buy a person's back with a paycheck, position, power or fear, a human being's genius, passion, loyalty, and tenacious creativity are volunteered only.
