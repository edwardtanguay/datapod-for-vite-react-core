[[
I have never been in the Scandanavian countries so in December I was thrilled to find out that the Copenhagen Marathon was in May and I had a good five months to train for it. I lost 5 kilos since February 1st and got my speed up and got my long runs up to 38K. The week before the race, I hurt my knee a bit on a half marathon training run and was worried that would be a problem in the marathon, but at 5K it was ok, then at 10K ok, 15K, half and 30K running fine all the way in. The weather was cool and perfect, just from 32K to 38K a downpour of sorts, a bit cold but also refreshing, and the Copenhagen streets lined with thousands of people who were cheering and screaming for you to keep running and finish. There were only 15,000 runners, so smaller than the major marathons but well organized, mostly through the old part of town, lots of left and right turns, a very memorable race.
